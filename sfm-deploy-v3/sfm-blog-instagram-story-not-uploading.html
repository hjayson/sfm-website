Why Won't My Instagram Story Upload?

J
Jayson HinesOctober 22, 2026 · 5 min read

If your Instagram Story will not upload, start by checking whether the problem is Instagram-wide, then test your connection, the app, and the photo or video. Change one thing at a time. That makes it easier to spot the cause and avoids deleting a draft you still need.

Is Instagram having a wider problem?

Before changing settings, see whether other Instagram features load and ask someone else if Stories are working for them. If several parts of the app are failing for more than one person, the issue may be temporary. Save your media and try again later instead of repeatedly tapping Share.

Does the Story upload on a different connection?

A connection can look fine while an upload stalls. Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or the other way around, and try once more. If the upload moves on one connection but not the other, restart your router or move to a steadier signal before retrying.

Could the photo or video file be causing it?

Test with a different photo or a short video. If that works, the original file may be too large, damaged, or in a format Instagram is struggling to process. Keep the original safe, then export a fresh copy from your photo or video editor and upload that version.

If the Story contains music, stickers, or several edits, try a plain copy of the same media. That can help narrow down whether one added element is involved. You can also review our guide to troubleshooting Instagram Reels that will not upload if Reels are failing too.

Is the Instagram app up to date and responding?

Close and reopen Instagram, then check your device's app store for an update. Restarting the phone can clear a temporary app problem. Before deleting or reinstalling Instagram, save the photo or video somewhere else and check whether you have drafts you want to keep.

Does your account show a restriction?

If uploads fail across different files and connections, review the account status and any notices Instagram has shown you. An account limitation can affect what you can share. Follow the instructions in the notice or use Instagram's help options if you think something was flagged by mistake.

How can a business avoid missing a time-sensitive Story?

Keep the original image or video outside the app until the Story is live. For an offer or announcement that matters after a day, publish the information somewhere customers can find later too, such as your feed, website, or social media marketing plan. A Story is useful for a quick update, but it should not be the only saved copy of an important message.

Common questions

Why won't my Instagram Story upload?
The connection, app, media file, or account status may be involved. Test each one separately and keep a copy of the original media.

How can I tell if Instagram is down?
Check whether other Instagram features work and ask someone else to test. If several features fail for multiple people, wait and try again later.

Why does my Story get stuck uploading?
A weak connection, temporary app problem, or media file issue can interrupt the upload. Try a different connection and a smaller or different file.

Will reinstalling Instagram delete my drafts?
It may remove locally saved drafts or app data. Save media separately and check what is backed up before reinstalling.

What should a business do if a Story still will not post?
Save the original media, try a different connection or device, review account status, and report a persistent issue through Instagram's help options.
