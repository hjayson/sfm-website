How to Get More Comments on Instagram Posts

J
Jayson Hines October 30, 2026 · 6 min read

If you want more comments on Instagram, give people something specific and easy to respond to. A useful question tied to the photo, Reel, or customer problem works better than a vague “Thoughts?” because people can see what kind of answer you’re inviting.

Why do people scroll past a caption question?

Most people are moving quickly. If a caption ends with a broad question like “What do you think?”, they have to do the work of figuring out what to say. They may agree with the post and keep scrolling anyway.

Make the response clear. A bakery could ask which seasonal pastry someone would try first. A home service company might ask what homeowners check before scheduling a repair. A dentist could answer a common question, then invite readers to share what still feels confusing.

The question should fit the post. Don’t tack on a prompt just to collect comments. If the answer would not help your audience or teach you something useful, leave it out.

How can you write a question people can answer?

Start with one topic from the post, then ask for one small response. A choice between two real options is easy to answer. So is a question about a familiar decision, a local preference, or a problem the reader may have faced.

For example, a Perrysburg contractor posting a before-and-after project could ask, “Which part of this entryway would you update first?” A restaurant could ask what lunch special people would bring back. The best prompt comes from the business and its customers, so use examples that are true to your work.

Keep the wording plain. Avoid asking for a comment, a tag, and a share all at once. One invitation is enough. And don’t make up a giveaway or promise a response you can’t provide.

What kinds of Instagram posts invite useful comments?

Show a decision, answer a question, or explain a small part of your process. A short video can show how a product is used. A carousel can compare two options or walk through a common step. A photo can show a finished project and give people a detail to notice.

Local businesses have an advantage here: customers know the place, the season, and the problems that come with living nearby. A lawn-care company in Northwest Ohio can talk about what to check after a wet week. A salon can explain how to prepare for an appointment. A credit union can clarify one point people often miss when comparing loan options.

Don’t rely on controversy or engagement bait. You want comments that create a real exchange, not a pile of one-word replies that have nothing to do with your business.

How should a business respond when comments come in?

Read the comment and answer the person, not a script. If someone asks a question, give a useful answer or point them to the right next step. If they share a preference, acknowledge it and ask a natural follow-up when that helps the conversation.

Move private details out of public replies. For questions about someone’s account, health, finances, or appointment, offer a safe way to continue privately. Keep your public response respectful and don’t pressure people to buy.

Set aside time to check the post after publishing. A question that gets no reply from the business can feel like a dead end. If comments are sparse, learn from the post and try a clearer prompt next time rather than posting the same request more often.

How can you tell whether the comments are helping?

Look beyond the raw count. Notice whether the people commenting are potential customers, whether they ask relevant questions, and whether the conversation leads to a profile visit, message, call, or booking. Those actions matter more than a number by itself.

Compare similar posts over time. Change one thing at a time, such as the question or format, so you can see what your audience responds to. Instagram’s features and reporting can change, so check the current account insights when you review results.

Common questions

What should I ask people to comment on Instagram?
Ask one specific question tied to the post, such as which option they would choose or what they look for before booking. Make it easy to answer in a sentence.

Do questions in Instagram captions get more comments?
A clear question gives readers a direct way to respond, but it cannot guarantee comments. The prompt should fit the post and matter to the people you want to reach.

How often should a business ask for comments?
Use a question when it adds something to the post. Asking for a response in every caption can feel repetitive, so vary the way you invite people into the conversation.

Should I reply to every Instagram comment?
When possible, reply to genuine comments with a useful answer. A thoughtful response shows people that someone is listening and can help keep the conversation helpful.

Are comments more important than leads?
Comments can show that a post started a conversation, but they are not the same as inquiries or sales. Track the response that matters to your business, such as calls, bookings, or qualified messages.
