The Mark

Clear Space Rule
Always maintain a minimum clear zone equal to the radius of the logo circle on all sides. Never crowd the mark with text, graphics, or other elements. The dashed ring represents the minimum exclusion zone.

04 · Voice & Messaging

Brand Voice

05 · Usage Rules

Do's & Don'ts

These rules protect the brand's integrity across every touchpoint, from ads to proposals to print.

✓

Do

✓
Use the official PNG logo file at all times
✓
Maintain the navy + orange color pairing, they work together
✓
Use Inter for all branded text (Bold 700 to 900 for headings)
✓
Lead with outcomes in all copy, results, growth, clients
✓
Keep the logo on dark navy or white backgrounds only
✓
Maintain the circular logo shape, never crop or reshape it
✓
Reference our Google rating and client count when building trust
✗

Don't

✗
Don't use green in any typography or UI elements
✗
Don't stretch, rotate, or add effects to the logo
✗
Don't place the logo on busy photos without a solid background
✗
Don't use off-brand colors like red, purple, or teal
✗
Don't use all-lowercase brand name ("sfm" or "sales funnel marketing")
✗
Don't use stock photography for SFM promotions, use real client work
✗
Don't use corporate/buzzword language in any client-facing copy
