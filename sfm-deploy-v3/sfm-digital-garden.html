Stop posting into the void.
Start building a community that actually buys.

Most local business owners are doing social media wrong — not because they're lazy, but because they've been given the wrong blueprint. They're broadcasting when they should be conversing. The Core Framework

Social media is a garden.
Here's how to tend it.

Every concept in the book maps to something you'd find in a real garden. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.

🌱
Soil
Your Brand Foundation
Your values, your voice, and the real reason you show up. Bad soil = nothing grows, no matter how much you water.
🌰
Seeds
First Conversations
A comment. A reply. A share. Every relationship starts with one small, genuine moment of connection.
💧
Watering
Consistent Engagement
Showing up. Responding. Caring about people's posts — not just your own. This is where most businesses fail.
☀️
Sunlight
Generous Value-Giving
Sharing knowledge, spotlighting others, and giving before you ever ask for anything. Generosity is the growth accelerator.
🌿
Weeds
Vanity Metrics & Comparison
Chasing likes. Obsessing over follower counts. Comparing your page to competitors. These kill real growth.
🍂
Seasons
The Rhythm of Growth
Some months explode. Some months are quiet. Learning to trust the process — without panicking — is a skill.
🛠️
Garden Tools
AI & Scheduling Tech
Tools help. But the gardener still has to show up. AI works for you — not instead of you.
🌾
The Harvest
Referrals, Fans & Revenue
When you've planted, watered, and tended well — the harvest comes. Loyal customers who buy again and bring friends.

About the Author

Jayson Hines

Founder, Sales Funnel Marketing | Perrysburg, Ohio

Jayson Hines founded Sales Funnel Marketing in 2017 after selling his insurance agency — which means he knows exactly what it feels like to start over and build something from nothing. He built his agency not through cold calls or paid ads, but through genuine relationships and showing up consistently for the businesses in his community.

Over the past seven years, he's grown Networks Cafe to over 1,600 members, run monthly AI workshops where 20–40% of attendees become clients, and helped over 127 local businesses in Northwest Ohio build social media presences that generate real leads and real revenue.

The Digital Garden is the book he wished he'd had when he was starting out — practical, honest, and written specifically for the business owner who's tired of advice that only works for tech startups in Silicon Valley.
