Why Is My Google Business Profile Post Not Showing?

J
Jayson Hines October 8, 2026 · 5 min read

Your Google Business Profile post may not be visible because it is still pending, Google did not approve it, or the profile is not verified. Open the post status first. Google says Pending posts do not show on Search or Maps, and Not approved posts stay hidden too.

That status tells you where to look next. You do not need to keep publishing the same update and hope one sticks. Check the profile, check the post, then fix the cause Google shows.

Why is my Google Business Profile post not showing?

Google reviews posts after you publish them. While a post is uploading or processing, its status can remain Pending. An unverified profile can also keep a post from showing. If Google marks it Not approved, the post is not visible on Search or Maps.

Open the profile you manage, choose the relevant location, and look at the post status. The Google Business Profile Help page explains the difference between Live, Pending, and Not approved. A Live post is public on Search and Maps, while the other two are not.

What does Pending or Not approved mean?

Pending means the upload or review is still underway, or your profile needs verification. Confirm that the profile is verified and give the post time to finish processing. Google does not give one guaranteed review time for every post, so avoid promising yourself it will appear after a set number of hours.

Not approved means the post did not meet a content rule. Select the status details or question mark beside it. That should point you to the issue. Update the copy, image, or link that caused the problem before you post it again.

Live means Google considers it public. Make sure you are looking at the right business location and its Updates or Posts section. Search results can vary, so check the profile itself before you assume the post disappeared.

Could an unverified or restricted profile be the problem?

Yes. Google says a pending post may be held because the profile is unverified. If you also see a notice that access or content is restricted, review that notice in the profile manager. A restriction on the profile can affect whether its content appears.

For an eligible restriction, Google provides an appeals tool. Read the notice and gather the documents it asks for before you submit an appeal. Google says not to send another appeal for the same issue while it is waiting for a decision.

What should I check in the post itself?

Read the full post as a customer would. Check that the text accurately describes your business, the image belongs with the update, and the link goes to a trustworthy page. Google notes that a phone number in the post description might be rejected if it cannot verify the number is connected to the business.

Posts about regulated products or services have additional limits. Keep the update focused on helpful business information and avoid adding restricted product details. If Google shows a specific policy issue, fix that issue instead of rewriting unrelated parts of the post.

If the post is Live but its photo is missing, that may be a separate image issue. Our guide to Google Business Profile photos that are not showing covers what to check next.

How do I get the post visible again?

Start with the status. If it is Pending, confirm profile verification and allow processing to finish. If it is Not approved, read the policy details, make the requested change, and submit a clean version. If it is Live, check the exact location and look at the profile’s Updates or Posts section.

Keep a copy of the original text and image before editing. That makes it easier to see what changed if Google flags the revised post too. When you need support, include the business location, the post status, and the specific message Google displayed.

Common questions

How long does a Google Business Profile post stay pending?
Google says Pending means the post is uploading or processing, or the profile is not verified. Check verification first. If it remains pending after processing is complete, use the status details and Google Business Profile Help to decide what to do next.

Can customers see a post marked Not approved?
No. Google says a Not approved post does not show on Search or Maps. Open the status details to see the policy issue, then edit the post so it follows the rules before trying again.

What if my post says Live but I cannot find it?
Google describes Live as public on Search and Maps. Check the correct business location and open its Updates or Posts section. Search can look different across devices and accounts, so verify the location before making another post.

Can a phone number cause a post to be rejected?
It can. Google says a phone number in the post description might be rejected if Google cannot confirm it is connected to that business. Use the profile phone field or a link to your website instead.

Where can I appeal a restricted Business Profile post?
Use Google’s Business Profile appeals tool if the restriction is eligible for appeal. Review the policy notice and gather the requested evidence first. Do not submit duplicate appeals while one is being reviewed.
