Why Can't I Add Music to My Instagram Story?

J
Jayson HinesOctober 13, 2026 · 5 min read

If you can't add music to an Instagram Story, the cause may be a licensing limit tied to your account, the track, or your region. It can also be a temporary app issue. First check whether the Music sticker is missing or just one song is unavailable. Those point to different causes.

Why can't you add music to your Instagram Story?

Instagram's music library isn't identical for every account, post type, or location. Meta says its licensed music library is intended for personal, non-commercial use, and that certain business accounts and certain kinds of posts may not have access. Music can also be unavailable in some regions. Instagram's explanation of music library access lays out those limits.

Is the Music sticker missing, or is one song unavailable?

If the Music sticker itself is gone, check that you're making a Story in the Instagram app and that the app is current. If the sticker opens but one track can't be found or plays an unavailable notice, try another song. Rights can differ between tracks and formats. One missing song doesn't prove your whole account is broken.

If the problem started after an update, close Instagram fully and reopen it. Check your connection, then try again. If music is still missing across Stories, test another format or account only if you already have one available. That can help you tell whether the issue follows the account or the app.

Could your account or region limit the music library?

It could. Meta limits access to some licensed tracks for certain business accounts and post types, and says catalog access can differ by country or region. Don't change your account type just to chase a particular song. Review which business features you rely on before changing settings.

For more detail on business account music access, read why some Instagram business accounts can't use popular songs.

What should you try before reporting a problem?

Update Instagram, close and reopen it, and try the Music sticker again on a new Story draft. Search for a different track. If the sticker is missing everywhere, check your account and region against Instagram's help page. When those checks don't explain it, use Instagram's in-app report-a-problem option and describe what happens.

What can a business use when a track isn't available?

Meta's Sound Collection offers music and sounds cleared for commercial use, including ads. You can find it from the Story music sticker or the Reels camera, according to Instagram's help page. Stick to tracks provided for your intended use. A song appearing somewhere online doesn't automatically give your business permission to use it.

Common questions

Why is music missing from my Instagram Story?
The Music sticker or a song may be unavailable because of account or post eligibility, regional licensing, or a temporary app problem. Check whether all music is missing or only one track.

Why can't my business account find popular songs?
Instagram says certain business accounts and certain types of posts don't have access to its licensed music library because that library is intended for personal, non-commercial use. Availability can also vary by region.

Can I use music from Instagram in a business Story?
Use only music that Instagram makes available for your account and the kind of post you're publishing, and follow the applicable music terms. Meta's Sound Collection is its royalty-free option for commercial use.

Will switching account type restore Instagram music?
It may change which features you can access, but don't switch just to get one song. First check Instagram's current music guidance and consider whether you'd lose business tools that matter to you.

What should I do if only one song is unavailable?
Try another track. Music rights can differ by song, region, and format, so one unavailable track doesn't always mean the Story feature is broken.
