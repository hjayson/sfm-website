Questions, answered.

The stuff people ask before they say yes.

Do I need any equipment or experience?

None. We bring everything: the recorder, the mics, the boom arms, the headphones. You just sit down and talk. If you can hold a conversation, you can host a podcast with us.

Where do we record?

Your office, conference room, or wherever you are comfortable in the Toledo and Northwest Ohio area. We make it work in almost any room and handle the sound. Travel within about 30 miles of Perrysburg is included.

How long does a session take?

Usually two to three hours once a month. We record your month of episodes in that one sitting. On the Pro plan that is a full month of weekly content from a single afternoon.

What do I actually have to do?

Show up, talk, and approve the episodes. We handle naming, artwork, editing, clips, publishing, and the feed. Your only job is the conversation.

Can it be a video podcast too?

Yes. Every plan includes a clean video cut and social clips. Want a full broadcast-style multi-cam video podcast? That is a simple add-on starting at $500 per session.

How soon can we launch?

Once we have your launch details, most shows are live within a couple of weeks. Book a free call and we will map out your first month.
