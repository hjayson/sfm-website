How Do I Get More Followers on TikTok?

J
Jayson Hines November 2, 2026 · 6 min read

To get more followers on TikTok, make it clear who your videos are for and what a viewer will keep learning by following you. Pick questions your customers already ask, answer one at a time, and give each video a useful first line. A clear profile helps the right viewers decide whether to stick around.

Follower count is easy to watch. It can also distract you. A local business doesn't need every person on TikTok. It needs the people who may visit, book, call, or remember the business when the need comes up.

Why do people follow one TikTok account and skip another?

A viewer follows when they can guess what they will get from the next video. If one post is about a bakery's cupcakes, the next is a random dance, and the next is an unrelated sale, a new viewer may not see a reason to return.

That doesn't mean every video needs to look the same. Keep a recognizable point of view. A Perrysburg home contractor might show what to check before a winter repair, explain a common estimate question, or walk through a job detail customers usually miss. Each video earns its place by being useful to roughly the same kind of person.

How do you give the right viewer a reason to follow?

Start with a simple promise that matches what you can keep delivering. A dentist might answer everyday questions about appointments. A restaurant could show how a dish is made or explain what's on the menu this week. A service business can make short videos that help customers know when a repair needs attention.

Put that promise in your bio and make your pinned videos support it. When someone visits your profile, they should understand what the business does and what sort of posts they will see. How can a business find TikTok topics people care about?

Write down the questions customers ask before they buy, book, or call. Look for the parts that take time to explain in person. Those are often good video topics because viewers can recognize the problem right away.

If it's available to your account, TikTok's Creator Search Insights can show topics people search for on TikTok and content gaps. Treat those suggestions as research. Choose a topic only when your business can give a real answer. TikTok says availability may vary by region and account.

Keep a short list in your phone. When a customer asks a useful question, add it. You will have a better starting point than trying to invent a clever idea every time you want to post.

What should the first few seconds of a TikTok video do?

Tell people what the video answers before you explain the details. Say the question in ordinary language. Put the main idea on screen too, especially if someone is watching with the sound off.

Skip the long introduction. Start with the actual issue: “Here’s why your basement smells damp after it rains,” or “Three things to check before ordering a custom cake.” The rest of the video should deliver what the opening promised. If it takes longer, break the subject into a short series.

How can a repeatable video series help you earn follows?

A series gives a viewer a reason to come back. It also makes planning easier. Choose a useful format you can repeat, such as answering a weekly customer question or showing one step from a project.

Give each video a specific point. “Part two” alone doesn't tell a new viewer why to watch. Add a title that makes sense on its own, then refer to the earlier video when the context helps. Viewers should be able to find value even if they see the series out of order.

How do you invite viewers to follow without begging?

Ask when you have given them a reason. A brief line such as “Follow for more home maintenance answers” works when that's what your account actually shares. Avoid promising a result or schedule you cannot maintain.

Reply to comments with care. If a question needs a longer answer, it may become a useful next video. You can also respond with a video when the feature is available. The goal is to make the conversation helpful, not to chase every comment with a sales pitch.

Which TikTok numbers should you watch?

Look past the follower total. Check which videos people watch, share, save, and comment on, then see whether profile visits and follows move with those posts. TikTok's official explanation of its recommendation system describes several kinds of signals, including viewer interactions and information about the content. No single signal tells the whole story.

For a business, connect the platform activity to a real next step when you can. Ask new customers how they heard about you, use a trackable link, or compare inquiry patterns before and after a content series. Keep your expectations grounded. A video can be useful even if it doesn't take off overnight.

If your videos are getting very few views at all, that is a different problem from viewers watching but not following. Start with this TikTok no-views troubleshooting guide and check whether the post is public and eligible for discovery.

How often should you post to grow on TikTok?

Set a pace that leaves time to make the videos clear and answer comments. One useful post each week is a sensible starting point if that's what your team can keep up with. Review what people respond to, then adjust. A burst of rushed videos followed by silence is hard to sustain.

Keep a small bank of ideas and record a few when the location, product, or staff are ready. You don't need a complicated studio setup. Clear audio, enough light, and an honest answer matter more than polishing every second.

Common questions

How long does it take to get more TikTok followers?
There is no fixed timeline. Track whether the people who watch your videos visit your profile and follow, then keep improving the topics and formats that attract the right viewers.

Should my business follow trends to grow on TikTok?
Use a trend only when it fits your business and audience. A trend can help a video feel timely, but it won't explain what you offer or why someone should follow.

How often should a business post on TikTok?
Choose a pace you can maintain while making useful videos. A steady schedule gives you more chances to learn, but posting more often by itself doesn't guarantee follower growth.

Do hashtags get you more followers on TikTok?
Relevant hashtags can describe a video and help people understand its topic. Use a few that fit the post rather than stuffing the caption with broad tags.

Should I buy TikTok followers?
No. Purchased followers are unlikely to be interested in your business, and a larger number doesn't mean you have a useful audience. Focus on earning follows from people you can actually help.
