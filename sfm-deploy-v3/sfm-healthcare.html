The Real Problem

If This Sounds Familiar,
You're Not Alone.

Med spa owners and healthcare practitioners are exceptional at what they do — but social media and digital marketing is a completely different skill set. Here's what we hear from nearly every new client before they work with us.

🔎

You're Invisible Where Your Patients Are Looking

The people in your zip code who are ready to book a Botox appointment, laser treatment, or wellness consultation are scrolling Instagram and Facebook right now. If your practice doesn't show up with professional, trust-building content, they book with someone who does — and they don't come back.

⚖️

HIPAA Creates Real Anxiety Around Content

You know you can't post patient names or identifiable photos without proper consent. But the rules feel murky, and the last thing you want is to post something that creates a compliance issue. So instead, you post nothing — or only the safest, most generic content that doesn't actually attract anyone.

📵

Meta Limits What Healthcare Ads Can Show

Meta has strict policies around before/after imagery in healthcare and wellness ads. If you don't know the rules, your ads get rejected, your account gets flagged, and your ad spend evaporates with nothing to show for it. Most general agencies don't know these nuances. We do — and we build every campaign around compliant angles that still convert.

📅

Inconsistent Posting Kills Your Credibility

A potential patient visits your Instagram and sees the last post was three months ago. What does that say about your practice? In their mind: maybe they're not that busy, maybe they're not that good, maybe I should keep looking. Consistency isn't just a vanity metric — it directly affects whether a stranger trusts you enough to book.

🩺

The Hard Truth: Your Competitors Are Already Running Ads

While you're busy providing great patient care, the med spa or clinic down the road is spending money on Meta ads, posting professional Reels, and capturing every new-patient search in your area. The gap between you and them is not skill or quality. It's visibility. We close that gap.
