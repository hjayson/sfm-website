Google Business

The #1 Free Marketing Tool Most Perrysburg Businesses Are Ignoring

J
Jayson Hines April 22, 2025 · 3 min read

If someone searches "dentist near me" or "best pizza in Perrysburg" on Google right now, what shows up first? It's not a website. It's not an ad. It's Google Business Profiles — the map listings with photos, reviews, hours, and a phone number ready to tap.

This is the most underutilized free marketing tool in local business — and it's one of the first things we fix for every client at Sales Funnel Marketing.

What Is Google Business (and Why Does It Matter)?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the free listing that shows up when people search for local businesses on Google or Google Maps. It includes your business name, address, phone number, hours, photos, reviews, and posts.

When someone searches "hair salon near me" in Perrysburg, Google shows the top 3 local listings — called the "Local Pack" — before any organic website results. Getting into that top 3 can mean dozens of new calls per month without spending a dollar on ads.

The 5 Things Most Businesses Get Wrong

1. They set it up once and never touch it again. Google rewards active profiles. Businesses that post regularly, respond to reviews, and add new photos consistently rank higher. A profile that hasn't been updated since 2022 is being outranked by competitors who are more active — even if those competitors have fewer overall reviews.

2. They ignore reviews. 88% of consumers trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations. Responding to every review — positive or negative — signals to Google and to potential customers that you're engaged and professional. Not responding to a bad review is often worse than the bad review itself.

3. Wrong or incomplete information. Old phone numbers. Missing hours. No website link. These seem small but they cost you calls every single week. Google also penalizes listings with inconsistent information across the web.

4. No photos — or terrible photos. Businesses with 10+ photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks. Phone photos are fine. Just make sure they're well-lit, current, and show your space, team, and work.

5. They don't use Google Posts. Google lets you post updates, offers, and events directly to your listing. Most businesses never use this feature. The ones that do show up more prominently and give potential customers a reason to choose them over a competitor.

What to Do This Week

If you haven't logged into your Google Business Profile in the last 30 days, do it today. Update your hours if anything has changed. Add at least 3 new photos. Post an update — an offer, a recent project, a team spotlight, anything. Then ask your last 5 satisfied customers to leave you a review.

That's an hour of work that can generate calls for months. Most of your competitors won't bother — which is exactly why you should.
