Common Questions

FAQs for Restaurant Owners

Do I need to hire a photographer?

Not necessarily. We guide you on how to shoot great food content with an iPhone, or we can coordinate with a photographer if your budget allows. Great food content doesn't have to be expensive.

How do Facebook ads work for restaurants?

We target people within your service area who match your customer profile — age, interests, behaviors. We run offers or awareness campaigns that drive them through your door.

Can you help with catering or private events too?

Absolutely. We can build dedicated campaigns for your catering arm, private dining room, or corporate events that are separate from your dine-in traffic strategy.

What if I'm a food truck or pop-up?

We love food trucks. Your mobility is actually an advantage on social — we'll build a following that tracks you wherever you go.
