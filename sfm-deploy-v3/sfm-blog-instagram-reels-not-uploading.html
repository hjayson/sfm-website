Why Won't My Instagram Reels Upload?

J
Jayson HinesOctober 16, 2026 · 5 min read

If your Instagram Reel won't upload, check the connection, app, video file, and account status in that order. First make sure the Reel didn't already publish, and confirm your draft is saved before you troubleshoot.

Why is my Instagram Reel stuck uploading?

A stalled upload can come from a spotty connection, a temporary app problem, a large or damaged video file, or an account issue. One failed upload doesn't tell you which one it is. Start with the checks that won't put your draft at risk.

Look at your profile's Reels tab before trying again. If the Reel is there, the upload may have completed even if the app showed an error. If it's not there, check drafts and keep the original video on your phone.

How can I tell whether the problem is my connection?

Load another page or play a video in a different app. If those struggle too, fix the connection first. Move to a more stable network if one is available, then reopen Instagram and check the draft before retrying.

Try one upload attempt and give it time to finish. Tapping Share over and over can leave you unsure which attempt is still running.

What should I check in the Instagram app?

Update Instagram if an update is available, then close and reopen the app. Check that your phone has room to save and process the video. If the upload issue started after an app update, note that detail because it may help when you report the problem.

Don't delete the app or clear its data until you've confirmed that drafts you care about are saved somewhere else. Draft storage can depend on your device and app state.

Could the video file be the reason my Reel won't post?

It could be. If other Reels upload normally, try a short copy of the same video or a different clip. That helps you see whether the issue follows one file. Keep the original and avoid making edits that overwrite it until the test is done.

If the Reel uploads but looks soft or blurry afterward, use this separate guide to fix blurry Instagram Reels. A quality problem after publishing has different checks from a failed upload.

What if every Reel upload keeps failing?

Check Instagram's Account Status and notifications for a restriction or review message. If you see one, follow the in-app steps shown there. If different videos fail on more than one stable connection, use Instagram's Help section to report the issue and include the error message.

Common questions

Why is my Instagram Reel stuck uploading?
A weak or interrupted connection, a large video file, an app problem, or a temporary Instagram issue can stop an upload. Check whether the Reel appears on your profile before trying again.

Will I lose my Reel if I close Instagram?
It depends on whether Instagram saved the video as a draft. Confirm that the draft is saved before closing or reinstalling the app, and keep a separate copy of the original video when possible.

How do I know if my Reel actually posted?
Open your profile and check the Reels tab, then view the post from another account if you can. An error message alone does not confirm whether the upload finished.

Could my internet connection stop a Reel upload?
Yes. Try loading another page or video, then retry on a stable connection. Avoid repeatedly tapping Share while the first attempt may still be running.

What if none of my Reels will upload?
Check Instagram's Account Status and notifications for restrictions, update the app, and use Instagram's in-app Help option to report the issue if it continues across different videos and connections.
