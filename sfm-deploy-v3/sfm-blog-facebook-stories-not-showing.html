Why Are My Facebook Stories Not Showing?

J
Jayson Hines October 21, 2026 · 6 min read

If your Facebook Story is not showing, first work out whether it is missing for you or for other people. Then check the account you posted from, the Story audience, muted Stories, and the app connection. Those checks usually tell you whether this is a visibility setting or a temporary loading issue.

What do you mean when you say the Story is not showing?

There are a few different problems people describe this way. You may not see the Stories row in your feed. Your own Story may be missing from your profile. Or you may see it while a friend or customer cannot.

Try to name the exact symptom before changing settings. Open the profile or Page that shared the Story. If it appears there but not in your feed, focus on feed loading and muted Stories. If it is missing everywhere, check whether it finished publishing and whether you are in the right profile.

Why is my own Facebook Story missing?

Start with the account switcher. Many people manage a personal profile and one or more Pages, and it is easy to share from the wrong place. Confirm the profile or Page name, then check its Story archive or recent activity to see whether the Story was posted.

If you posted from a Page, check that you have the right Page access and that you are viewing the Page in the same way you used to publish. Facebook's Page tools and labels can vary by device and account setup. The Facebook Page access guide explains the different kinds of access.

Also check for an upload or publishing error. A weak connection, a stalled upload, or an app that needs an update can leave you unsure whether the Story went live. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, close and reopen Facebook, and check again before uploading a second copy.

Why can other people not see my Facebook Story?

The Story audience is one of the first things to check. Facebook has privacy controls for Stories, and a Story shared with a limited audience will not be visible to everyone who visits your profile. Open the Story settings and review who can see it, including any exclusions.

Facebook also lets people mute another account's Stories. If one person cannot see yours, ask them to check whether your Stories are muted and to visit your profile directly. If several people cannot see it, check your own audience and account notices first.

Facebook's privacy settings guide lists Story controls under Audience and visibility. The exact labels may shift between the app and desktop, so look for the Story audience setting rather than relying on an old screenshot.

What should you check before you post the Story again?

Confirm the Story appears on the profile that created it. Check whether the audience is limited. Ask one trusted person in the intended audience to look from their own account. If it is a Page Story, verify that you are in the correct Page and have access to manage its content.

Then test the app. Refresh the feed, update Facebook if an update is available, and try a different connection or device. If the problem is only on one device, you have narrowed it down. If the Story is missing across devices, look at the audience, publishing status, and any notice on the account.

Common questions

Why can I see a Facebook Story but my friend cannot?
The Story's audience may not include your friend, or they may have muted your Stories. Check the audience selected for that Story and ask your friend to look for a muted account setting.

Why are Facebook Stories not showing at the top of my feed?
The Stories row may not have loaded, the app may be having a temporary problem, or Stories may be muted. Refresh Facebook, reopen the app, and check whether the Story appears on the person's profile.

Can a Facebook Story be hidden from some people?
Yes. Facebook's Story privacy controls let an account choose who can see a Story. Review the audience and exclusion settings in Facebook's privacy controls.

Why does my Facebook Story disappear after I post it?
First confirm it finished sharing and that you are looking at the right account or Page. If it appeared and then disappeared, check the Story archive and account notices before uploading it again.

Should I delete and repost a Facebook Story that is missing?
Not right away. Check the audience, account, app connection, and whether the Story is visible from another device. Reposting before you know the cause can create duplicates without fixing the setting.
