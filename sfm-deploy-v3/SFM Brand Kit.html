02Visual Identity

Logo System

Our logo is our most recognizable asset. These are the only two approved variants. Never stretch, recolor, add effects, or crowd the mark.

White, On Dark Backgrounds
Black, On Light Backgrounds
02.1Clear Space

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the mark on all sides. No type, graphics, or other elements enter this zone, ever.

← Minimum clear space = height of the mark, all sides →

Always Do
  • Use the white logo on navy, dark, or photographic backgrounds
  • Use the black logo on white, cream, or light neutral backgrounds
  • Maintain minimum clear space on all sides at all times
  • Scale proportionally, width and height locked together always
  • Reproduce only from the approved files provided in this kit
Never Do
  • Recolor the logo with non-brand colors or apply gradients
  • Add drop shadows, glows, outlines, bevels, or any effects
  • Rotate, skew, stretch, or distort the logo in any direction
  • Place on low-contrast, busy, or patterned backgrounds
  • Display smaller than 80px wide in digital, 0.75" in print
