Why It Exists

Networking that actually
feels good to show up to

Most networking is a room full of people waiting for their turn to pitch. NetWORKs Café is the opposite — a friendly, recurring space built on giving first. Here's what you get out of it.

🤝
Real relationships
Show up monthly and the same faces become friends, collaborators, and referral partners. Trust compounds over time.

How a Meetup Works

A typical NetWORKs Café

Casual, friendly, and always done on time. We meet in person at 8:00 AM on the first Wednesday — here's the flow of a typical meetup.

First 15 min
Coffee & Connections
Doors open at 8:00 AM. Grab a coffee, say hello, and settle in. Check in at the sign-in sheet so we can keep everyone connected after — and add the folks you meet in the Facebook group.
Arrive Sign in Casual chat
Next 20 min
Around-the-Room Intros
Everyone gets 30 seconds: who you are, what you do, and one thing you're looking for or can help with. Short, friendly, no pressure — by the end, the whole room knows you.
30-second intro Your ask Your offer
Next 20 min
Topic of the Month
A short, practical segment on growing your business — social media, AI tools, local marketing, referrals, and more. Useful enough to take back to your desk that afternoon.
Practical Q&A Takeaways
Last 15 min
Open Networking
Follow up with the people you want to talk to, swap contact info, and make a plan to connect before next month. We wrap on time, every time.

Common Questions

FAQ

When and how often does NetWORKs Café meet?
The 1st Wednesday of every month. You can join online or in person, whichever works for you that month.
Is it really free?
Yes — completely free to attend. No membership fees, no contracts, no catch. Just RSVP and show up.
Where do you meet?
We meet in person at 8:00 AM on the first Wednesday of every month, at a different local spot each time. Sign up for alerts and we'll send you the exact address before each meetup. Between meetups, the community lives in our Facebook group.
What kind of businesses come?
All kinds — local service businesses, retailers, freelancers, consultants, and side-hustlers. If you're building something and want to grow it through real relationships, you'll fit right in.
Do I need to be a Sales Funnel Marketing client?
Nope. NetWORKs Café is open to everyone. It's hosted by Jayson, but it's a community first — come for the connections, not a sales pitch.
How do I find out where we're meeting next?
Sign up for alerts above and we'll send you the date, time, and address before each meetup, plus a reminder. You can also check the Facebook group or email Jayson anytime.
