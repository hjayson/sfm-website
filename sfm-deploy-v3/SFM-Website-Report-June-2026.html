The wins

What we need to fix

In priority order. These are the gaps between "getting traffic" and "getting leads."

Priority 1
Turn on conversion tracking. Your "Key Events" in GA4 read 0. That means we can see traffic but not leads. We have no idea how many of those 368 visitors filled out a form, clicked to call, or booked. Setting up a few key events (form submit, call click, booking) is the single most valuable fix, it turns this from a traffic report into a leads report.
Priority 1
The 404 page is your #2 most-viewed page. 49 views landed on "Page Not Found." Something is sending real people and bots to dead URLs (old links, a broken menu item, or outdated pages without redirects). We should find what is linking to those dead URLs and add redirects so that traffic lands somewhere useful instead of bouncing.
Priority 2
Filter out bot and overseas traffic. Singapore is your #2 country, with India, Vietnam, and China close behind. For a Northwest Ohio local business, that is almost certainly bot and scraper traffic inflating the numbers. Adding a bot filter means your reports reflect real local prospects, so the 368 number becomes trustworthy.
Priority 2
Tag your links so "Direct" makes sense. Direct is 60% of traffic but only 20% engaged and 7 seconds on average. Some of that is bots, and some is untagged links from your Google Business Profile, the app, QR codes, and emails. Adding UTM tags to those links tells us which efforts are actually driving visits, instead of dumping everything into a "Direct" bucket we cannot read.
Priority 3
Grow organic search. It is only 11% of traffic but your highest quality channel. Keep publishing local and service content (the Google Maps blog and city pages are good examples) to pull more of these high-intent visitors.
Priority 3
Fix the Analytics default and clean up junk pages. GA opens to an empty "Visits" property by default, which makes it look like you have zero traffic. Star "Sales Funnel Site - GA4" so it always loads the real data. Separately, set the utility and thank-you pages (especially the leftover test-probe page) to "noindex" so they never show up in search.
