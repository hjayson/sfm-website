How Do I Add a Link to My Instagram Bio?

J
Jayson Hines October 25, 2026 · 4 min read

If you want people to visit your website from Instagram, add the address through your profile's Links setting. Open your profile, tap Edit profile, choose Links, then add the external link and save. A URL typed into the regular bio text may show as plain text, so check the profile after saving.

How do I add a website link to my Instagram bio?

Open Instagram and go to your profile. Tap Edit profile, then Links, and choose Add external link. Paste the full web address, add a short title people will understand, and save.

Instagram changes labels from time to time, so the words may look a little different in your version of the app. If you do not see Links, update Instagram and look again in Edit profile.

Why is my Instagram link not clickable?

The most common fix is to put the address in the Links area instead of typing it in the bio description. Then save your profile and test the result. Open your profile from another account or a private browser window. That helps catch a link that only appears to work while you are signed in.

Check that the address starts with https://, that the destination page loads, and that there are no accidental spaces at the beginning or end. If the link opens the wrong page, edit the link itself and save again.

What link should a local business put first?

Send visitors to the page that matches the next step you mention in your posts. If a post invites people to schedule, use the booking page. If you are showing a service, link to that service page. A general homepage can work, though a focused page usually makes the next step clearer.

For a Toledo area business, make sure the page tells visitors what you do, where you serve, and how to contact you. Keep the link title plain. “Book an appointment” or “See our services” is easier to understand than a string of keywords.

How can I tell whether people use the link?

Use a link to a page that you can check in your website analytics, or add a tracking tag to the address if you already use campaign tracking. You can also ask new customers how they found you. Keep the method consistent so you can compare activity over time.

Do not change the destination every few days without a reason. If a promotion ends, update the link and check that the old post still points people toward a useful next step.

How should I connect my bio link to my social content?

Tell people what they will find when they tap it. If your post is about one specific offer, make the first link or landing page match that offer. If you want a broader overview, use a page with a few clear choices. Keep the path short and test it on a phone before you send people there.

Common questions

Where do I add a clickable link on Instagram?
Open your profile, choose Edit profile, then Links. Add the website address there and save it.

Can I add a link if I have a personal account?
Instagram provides profile links in its Links settings. If you do not see the option, update the app and check the account settings again.

Why is my Instagram bio link not clickable?
A web address typed into the bio text may display as plain text. Add it through the Links setting, save, then check the profile from another account.

Can I add more than one link to my Instagram profile?
Instagram profile settings may let you add multiple external links. Put the most useful next step first and give each link a clear title.

What should my Instagram bio link point to?
Send people to a page that matches what your posts invite them to do, such as book, shop, request information, or read more about a service.
