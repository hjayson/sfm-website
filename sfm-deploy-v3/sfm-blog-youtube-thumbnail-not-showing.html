Why Is My YouTube Thumbnail Not Showing Up?

J
Jayson Hines September 22, 2026 · 7 min read

Short answer: if the thumbnail you just uploaded still isn't showing, it's almost always one of two things. Either YouTube hasn't finished pushing the new image out yet and you're looking at a cached copy, or your channel isn't phone verified so the custom thumbnail never actually got applied. The first one fixes itself in about 30 minutes. The second one takes about two minutes to fix and then it's done forever.

I get this one from business owners a lot, usually right after they paid someone to make a nice thumbnail. They upload it, the video goes live, and the video still shows some random blurry freeze frame of them mid-blink. Frustrating, and it costs you clicks. So let's sort out which version of the problem you've got.

Did the thumbnail actually upload, or did YouTube reject it?

First thing to check. Open YouTube Studio on a desktop, go to Content, click the video, and look at the thumbnail section. If your custom image is sitting there selected, it uploaded fine and you've got a display problem. If you don't even see the option to upload a custom thumbnail, that's your answer right there and you can skip to the verification section below.

If the upload got rejected outright, it's usually the file. YouTube wants 1280 by 720 pixels minimum, a 16:9 shape, and under 2MB. JPG, PNG, GIF, and BMP all work. That 2MB ceiling is the one that trips people up, because a big PNG exported straight out of Canva or Photoshop can blow past it easily. Save it as a JPG and the size problem usually goes away.

Why does my video still show the old thumbnail?

Because you're looking at a cached copy. This is the most common version of the whole problem and it isn't really a problem at all.

When you swap a thumbnail, YouTube has to push that new image out across a lot of servers, plus your browser is holding onto the old one, plus anywhere the video is embedded is holding onto its own copy. So you get this window where the new thumbnail shows in Studio but the old one shows on the watch page, in search, and on your website. Give it 10 to 30 minutes. Most of the time it just resolves.

If you want to check whether it's really updated instead of waiting and guessing, open the video in an incognito window or on your phone using cell data instead of wifi. That pulls a fresh copy instead of whatever your browser saved. If it looks right there, you're fine, everybody else is already seeing the new one and your own browser is just being stubborn. Clear your cache or hard refresh and move on.

The one place it lingers longer is embeds. If that video is embedded on your website, the old thumbnail can hang around for a while because your site or your CDN cached it too. Clear your site cache and it'll catch up.

Why can't I upload a custom thumbnail at all?

If YouTube Studio isn't even giving you the custom thumbnail option, your channel almost certainly isn't verified. Custom thumbnails are an intermediate feature, and YouTube gates those behind phone verification to keep bots from flooding the place.

Go to youtube.com/verify, put in your phone number, type in the code they text you, and you're done. Then go to Settings, Channel, Feature eligibility, and open Intermediate features to confirm it turned on. Sometimes it's instant and sometimes it takes a few minutes and a refresh.

One other thing that kills custom thumbnails: an active Community Guidelines strike. If your channel has one, thumbnail upload gets pulled as part of the penalty and it comes back when the strike expires, which is usually 90 days, or sooner if you appeal and win. Same goes for a thumbnail that got flagged on its own. If you uploaded something that YouTube read as misleading or inappropriate, it'll strip it and drop back to an auto-generated frame without making a big announcement about it.

Why are no thumbnails loading for me anywhere on YouTube?

Different problem. If every thumbnail on YouTube is showing up gray or blank, including other people's videos, that's on your end.

Ad blockers and privacy extensions are the usual suspect. YouTube's anti-adblock scripts fight with them and thumbnails get caught in the crossfire. Open YouTube in an incognito window with extensions off. If the thumbnails come back, you found it, and you can whitelist YouTube in the extension.

After that it's the boring stuff. A weak connection or an app that hasn't updated in a year. Restricted Mode will also hide images it decides are questionable, so check that too. Clear the app cache on your phone and restart it. One of those usually does it.

Does changing a thumbnail hurt the video?

No, and you should change them more than you do. Swapping a thumbnail doesn't reset the algorithm or wipe your ranking. Views and watch time stay exactly where they were.

What actually happens is YouTube keeps serving the video and watches whether the new image gets a better click rate. If it does, you get more impressions. If it doesn't, you're right where you started. So a video that got decent reach but nobody clicked is a good candidate for a new thumbnail, and there's basically no downside to trying.

For a local business this matters more than people think. Your thumbnail is what shows up in search and every time somebody shares your link. A blurry auto-generated freeze frame makes a real company look like it doesn't care. If your videos are getting impressions but almost no clicks, the thumbnail is the first thing I'd look at, and then I'd read up on why YouTube videos stop getting views for the rest of it. What to do right now

Quick order of operations. 1: Open Studio on desktop and confirm the thumbnail is actually attached to the video. 2: If there's no custom thumbnail option, verify your phone at youtube.com/verify. 3: If it's attached but not showing, check it in incognito or on cell data before you do anything else. 4: Still stuck after an hour, re-save the image as a JPG under 2MB at 1280 by 720 and upload it again.

That covers almost all of them. The rare leftover is a strike or a flagged image, and YouTube Studio will tell you about that if you scroll down to the video's restrictions.

Common questions

How long does it take for a new YouTube thumbnail to show up?
Usually 10 to 30 minutes. YouTube has to push the new image out and your browser is holding an old copy, so check it in an incognito window or on cell data to see whether it's really updated.

Why is there no custom thumbnail option in YouTube Studio?
Your channel isn't verified. Custom thumbnails are an intermediate feature that requires phone verification. Go to youtube.com/verify, enter your number and the code they text you, then check Settings, Channel, Feature eligibility.

What size does a YouTube thumbnail need to be?
At least 1280 by 720 pixels, 16:9 shape, and under 2MB. JPG, PNG, GIF, and BMP all work. The 2MB limit is what usually blocks people, so saving as a JPG instead of a PNG fixes most rejected uploads.

Does changing a thumbnail hurt my video's ranking?
No. Your views and watch time stay put. YouTube keeps serving the video and measures whether the new image gets a better click rate, so a video with impressions but few clicks is a good one to swap.

Why are no thumbnails loading on YouTube at all?
That's on your device, not your channel. Ad blockers and privacy extensions are the usual cause, so try incognito with extensions off. After that check your connection, update the app, clear the app cache, and turn off Restricted Mode.
