How Do I Make a Facebook Post Shareable?

J
Jayson HinesOctober 15, 2026 · 5 min read

If people can't share your Facebook post, check the audience first. A post set for a limited group of people may not be visible to everyone they try to share it with. If the post is already public, check where you're trying to share it and whether that place allows it.

Why can't someone share my Facebook post?

Most often, the post's audience is too narrow. A profile post shared with friends, for example, stays within the limits of that audience. Sharing the link does not make the post public or give new people permission to see it.

Meta's guide to sharing on Facebook says the audience selector controls who can see a post. Check that setting on the post itself before changing anything else.

How do I check the audience setting?

Open the post on your profile and look for its audience label or the post menu. Review who can see it. If you want anyone to be able to view it, choose Public when that option is available and fits your privacy needs. The setting may vary by account and post type.

After changing the audience, copy the post link and test it in a private browser window, or ask someone outside your usual friend list to open it. That helps confirm what other people can actually see.

What if I published it from a Facebook Page?

Check that the Page post is published and that you're sharing the Page post itself. Page posts are generally intended for public viewing, but a destination such as a group can apply its own rules. A group may restrict links, promotional posts, or posts from Pages.

If sharing works to your profile but not to one group, the group may be the issue. Review its rules or ask an admin. You can also copy the post link and share it somewhere the group allows.

Could the post type or its original source be the problem?

It can be. A post shared from a private group, another person's profile, or a restricted event may keep the original limits. Some content also has sharing options that differ by format. Try sharing a regular text or photo post as a quick comparison.

If people can see the post but your business updates still aren't getting in front of them, the issue may be reach rather than sharing permissions. Here's a related guide on why Facebook posts may not be reaching followers.

What should I try if the post is public but still won't share?

Check whether the problem happens with one post or every post. Try copying the link, updating the Facebook app, and opening the post in a browser. If only one destination blocks it, review that destination's rules. If every public post fails, use Facebook's in-app help to report the issue.

For a business, make sure the post is published from the right Page and that the link opens for someone who isn't an admin. Small checks like that can save time before you rewrite the post. Common questions

Why can people not share my Facebook post?
The post may have a limited audience, or the place you are sharing it may not allow that kind of post. Check the audience setting first, then try sharing the post link.

How do I make a Facebook post public?
On a profile post, use the audience selector and choose Public if that fits what you want to share. Meta notes that the selector controls who can see the post.

Can someone share a post if they are not in the audience?
Usually they cannot give another person access to a post that the other person is not allowed to see. A share does not override the original audience.

Why can I share a post to a friend but not a group?
A group may have its own posting rules, and some post types or audiences may not be shareable there. Try copying the post link or ask a group admin about the group rules.

Can I change the audience after posting?
For many profile posts, you can use the post menu to edit the audience. The exact options can depend on the post and account.
