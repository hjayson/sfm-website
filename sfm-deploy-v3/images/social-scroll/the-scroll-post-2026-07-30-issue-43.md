## Caption

Your social posts can show up in Google Search now.

The catch: someone has to turn the data on.

Google Search Console now lets you connect Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube directly so you can see which Google searches are sending people to your social profiles.

You do not even need a website to use it.

That means an Instagram-only business, a TikTok-first creator, or a small local brand can finally see what people typed into Google before landing on a post.

This is free data.

It takes minutes to connect.

And if someone is managing SEO for your business, this is a fair question to ask them this week:

"Have you connected our social platform properties in Google Search Console yet?"

If the answer is no, that is an easy gap to close.
