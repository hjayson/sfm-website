## Caption

Meta may already be rewriting the words inside your ad images.

Not your caption.

Not your primary text.

The actual headline baked into the creative.

Meta's Advantage+ Creative can generate new versions of an uploaded image with different headline text while keeping the font, colors, layout, and design intact.

Those variants can be selected by default.

That makes this worth checking today, especially if you manage ads for a regulated industry or a brand that needs tight control over its messaging.

Open Ads Manager and review the Branding section under Identity. Add your logo, fonts, colors, tone, and restricted words. Then check your active ads for headline variants you did not write.

The feature could be useful when you choose to use it. The problem is discovering that Meta made the choice for you.
