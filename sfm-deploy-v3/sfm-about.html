Our Story

Built from scratch.
Grown through real relationships.

Jayson Hines didn't start as a marketer. He spent years running an insurance agency alongside building SFM — and when he finally closed the agency in 2023 to go all-in, he had one thing other agencies didn't: he knew exactly what it felt like to be the client.

He knew what it felt like to pour money into marketing that didn't convert. To post consistently and hear nothing back. To hire someone who sent you a report full of numbers that didn't translate to actual customers walking through the door.

"I built this agency the same way I help clients build their audiences — not by broadcasting, but by showing up, listening, and genuinely caring about the people in front of me."

Sales Funnel Marketing grew through real relationships: coffee meetings, local networking events, chambers of commerce, and the Networks Cafe community Jayson built to over 1,600 members. Not ads. Not cold outreach. People hiring people they actually trust.

Today, SFM manages social media, ads, and content for 127+ local businesses across Northwest Ohio — from restaurants in Toledo to contractors in Findlay to boutiques in Lima. And the philosophy hasn't changed one bit since day one.

How We Work

The principles we won't compromise on

These aren't marketing buzzwords. They're the actual reasons clients stay with us for years — not months.

01
Relationships First
We built this agency through real relationships, not cold calls. Every client is a neighbor — not a contract number. When you win, we win, and we take that personally.
02
Results Over Vanity
We don't celebrate follower counts. We celebrate phone calls, booked appointments, and revenue. If our work isn't putting money in your pocket, it's not working.
03
Local, On Purpose
We specifically serve Northwest Ohio businesses. Not because we can't go national — because we believe in building up our own community first. We shop at your stores. We eat at your restaurants.
04
Always Ahead of the Curve
Algorithms change. Platforms shift. AI is reshaping everything. We run monthly workshops and stay at the leading edge so your strategy is never six months behind the industry.
05
Radical Transparency
We tell you what's working and what isn't — in plain English. No reports full of numbers that don't mean anything. No hiding behind "it takes time."

The 90-Day Progress Plan
We set agreed benchmarks up front, report clearly, and review the next steps with you after the first 90 days.
