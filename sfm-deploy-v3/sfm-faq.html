Strategy
Platform choices, posting cadence, and content that actually converts.
Not all of them. Facebook and Instagram cover 90% of local business needs — massive reach, strong ad tools, every demographic. LinkedIn is worth it if you're B2B. TikTok is worth testing if your audience is under 40. You don't need to be everywhere. You need to be excellent where your customers actually are.
3–5 times per week on your main platforms is the sweet spot for most local businesses. Consistency beats frequency. Posting 3× a week every week crushes posting 10× a week for two weeks then going quiet for a month. We handle the scheduling so you never have to think about it.
It depends entirely on the platform — and that's exactly how we approach it. Instagram and TikTok reward short-form video: Reels, behind-the-scenes, before/after, real results. Facebook still performs well with a mix of video, carousels, and value-driven posts. LinkedIn favors thought leadership and proof-of-expertise content. We tailor the content format and style to each platform's algorithm — not one-size-fits-all posting. Real, authentic content almost always beats over-produced, stock-photo filler on every platform.
We create everything — copy, graphics, video editing, captions, hashtags. If you want to be involved (and some clients love being in the content), we'll make that happen. Most clients hand it off entirely and check in on the monthly report.

Ads vs. Organic
What actually moves the needle — and what's just spending money.
Boosting a post is a blunt instrument — Facebook shows it to more of your existing followers and some lookalikes. Real ad campaigns use Ads Manager: precise audience targeting, custom objectives (leads, purchases, calls), A/B testing, retargeting, and full analytics. Boosting spends your money. Real campaigns invest it. Most businesses wasting money on social media are boosting posts and wondering why nothing converts.
Organic builds trust. Ads drive volume. The businesses winning locally do both. Organic content is what makes a prospect trust you before they call. Ads are what gets them to see you in the first place. You can start with one — but the compounding effect of both together is where the real growth happens.
For local businesses, $500–$1,500/month in ad spend is a reasonable starting point. Enough to generate real data and real leads without overcommitting. We don't take a percentage of your ad spend — that's a conflict of interest. We charge a flat management fee and your ad budget goes 100% to the platforms.
