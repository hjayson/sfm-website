Privacy Policy

Sales Funnel Marketing · Perrysburg, Ohio

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Who We Are

Sales Funnel Marketing ("SFM," "we," "us," or "our") is a social media marketing agency located in Perrysburg, Ohio. We help local businesses grow through Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business strategies. Our website address is www.salesfunnelmarketing.us.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at jayson@salesfunnelmarketing.us or call 419-308-4576.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you:

  • Fill out a contact form or book a strategy call
  • Complete our social media score quiz or ROI calculator
  • Subscribe to our email list or newsletter
  • Communicate with us by phone, email, or social media

The types of information we may collect include your name, email address, phone number, business name, and any other information you choose to provide.

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs. This is standard web analytics data collected through tools like Google Analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule strategy calls
  • Provide marketing services to clients
  • Send you information about our services, case studies, and marketing tips (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Facebook & Meta Advertising

Our website uses the Meta Pixel (formerly Facebook Pixel), a piece of code that helps us understand how people interact with our website after seeing our Facebook or Instagram ads. The Meta Pixel may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

This data is used to measure the effectiveness of our advertising, create audiences for future ads, and improve our marketing. You can opt out of Meta's use of cookies for advertising by visiting Facebook Ad Preferences.

For more information about how Meta uses data, visit Meta's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your browsing experience and help us understand how our site is used. We use:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand visitor behavior (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Marketing cookies: Used to track visitors across websites for advertising purposes (e.g., Meta Pixel)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our business. These may include:

  • LeadConnector / GoHighLevel — for contact forms, appointment booking, customer relationship management, and website chat
  • FormSubmit — for forwarding contact form notifications by email
  • Google Analytics — for website analytics
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — for advertising and the Meta Pixel
  • Email marketing platforms — for newsletters and follow-up sequences

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data. We encourage you to review them.

Use of Artificial Intelligence

Sales Funnel Marketing uses artificial intelligence (AI) tools to assist in content creation, copywriting, research, strategy development, and business operations. This includes tools such as Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), and other AI-powered software platforms.

AI tools may be used to:

  • Draft, refine, or assist in producing social media content, captions, and marketing copy
  • Research industry trends, competitors, and audience insights
  • Assist with internal business workflows, reporting, and communications
  • Generate ideas and creative concepts for client campaigns

All AI-assisted content is reviewed, edited, and approved by our team before being published or delivered to clients. We do not input your confidential personal data into AI platforms without your knowledge. These AI platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. If you would like us to delete your information, please contact us at jayson@salesfunnelmarketing.us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jayson@salesfunnelmarketing.us.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
