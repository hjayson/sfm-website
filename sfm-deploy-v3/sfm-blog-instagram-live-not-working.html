Why Can’t I Go Live on Instagram?

J
Jayson Hines
October 26, 2026 · 5 min read

If you can’t go Live on Instagram, start with your account notice and Account Status. The problem may be eligibility or a feature restriction. If those look clear, check the app, phone permissions, and internet connection.

First, notice what’s happening. Is the Live option missing, or does a broadcast start and then fail? Those point to different fixes. Work through the checks below in order, and keep any error message you see.

Is Live missing, or does the broadcast fail?

If you can’t find Live in the creation menu, check Instagram’s current eligibility guidance. Platform rules can change, so use the notice shown in your account instead of relying on an old tutorial. If the option appears but the stream won’t start, move on to Account Status and your app settings.

If the stream starts and then stops, pay attention to the timing. A failure right away may come with an account notice. A stream that drops after a few minutes can point to a weak connection or a phone setting.

Can your account use Live right now?

Open your profile menu and look for Account Status. Instagram uses it to show whether account activity affects features you can use. Read the message there, including any review option or end date. Don’t keep retrying if Instagram says a restriction is active.

Also check Instagram’s Help Center for the current Live requirements. If your account is new or the app says it doesn’t meet a requirement, follow the in-app instructions. A friend’s account may have different access.

Could the app or your phone be blocking Live?

Update Instagram from your phone’s app store, close it fully, then open it again. Check your phone settings to make sure Instagram can use the camera and microphone. A Live broadcast needs both.

If you still can’t start, restart your phone and try once more. Before reinstalling Instagram, make sure you know your login details. You don’t want to fix one problem and get locked out of your account.

Is your connection strong enough to keep the stream going?

Try a different reliable network. If you’re on Wi-Fi, move closer to the router or test mobile data. If you’re on mobile data, check that your plan and phone settings allow Instagram to use it.

Close other apps that may be using the connection, then test again. If the broadcast still drops, write down when it happens and the exact message Instagram shows. That detail helps when you report a problem.

What should a local business check before trying again?

Test the camera and microphone before announcing a Live. Pick a spot with steady internet, and tell viewers what the session will cover. A short practice run can catch a quiet mic or poor lighting before customers join.

Common questions

Why can’t I go Live on Instagram?
Instagram Live may be unavailable because of current account eligibility, a feature restriction, or an app or connection issue. Read the notice in Instagram and check Account Status first.

Why is the Live option missing?
The Live option may not be available to every account. Check Instagram’s current eligibility guidance and Account Status, then update the app and reopen it.

Can Instagram restrict my access to Live?
Yes. Instagram may limit access to features. Account Status can show whether there is an account issue and what review steps are available.

What should I do if Instagram Live keeps stopping?
Update Instagram, check camera and microphone permissions, and test on a reliable Wi-Fi or mobile connection. If the issue continues, note any error message and report it through Instagram.

Should I reinstall Instagram if Live does not work?
Try the simpler checks first: update the app, restart it, confirm phone permissions, and check Account Status. Reinstall only after confirming your login details and that your account data is available.
