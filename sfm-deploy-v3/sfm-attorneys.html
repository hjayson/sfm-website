If This Sounds Familiar...

The Real Reason Your Phone
Isn't Ringing as Much as It Should

Exceptional legal work doesn't automatically generate exceptional visibility. These are the problems we hear from attorneys every single week.

🔍

Your competitors show up — you don't

You search "personal injury attorney Toledo" and three other firms appear before you do. Your reputation may be better, but you're invisible. In the digital world, invisible means unavailable — and that case goes to whoever showed up first in their feed or search results.

🕐

You have no time to post consistently

You're running a law practice, not a content studio. Between client calls, court dates, depositions, and paperwork, social media falls to the bottom of the list — or the intern handles it once a month. Inconsistency kills credibility online. Clients notice when your last post was six weeks ago.

⚠️

You're worried about bar association compliance

Marketing your law firm isn't like marketing a restaurant. Ohio bar rules govern what you can and cannot say in advertising — no guarantees of results, proper disclaimers, no misleading solicitation. Most general marketing agencies don't know these rules. One careless post can become an ethics issue.

📞

Referrals alone won't fill your caseload

Referrals are great — but they're unpredictable. You can't scale a firm on hoping former clients mention your name. The attorneys who are growing predictably have a digital pipeline running 24/7 that generates leads while they sleep, while they're in court, and while they're with family on weekends.

🏢

Generic marketing agencies don't understand legal

You've probably talked to an agency that promised results and delivered templated posts about "legal tips" that could have come from any firm in any state. They didn't understand your practice area, your local market, your client psychology, or the difference between a personal injury case and an estate planning inquiry. SFM is different — we build campaigns specific to your practice area, your geography, and the exact clients you want.
