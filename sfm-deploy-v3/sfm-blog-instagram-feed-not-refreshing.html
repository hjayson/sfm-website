Why Is My Instagram Feed Not Refreshing?

J
Jayson Hines November 5, 2026 · 6 min read

If your Instagram feed won’t refresh, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, close and reopen the app, then check whether Instagram loads on another device. The “Couldn’t refresh feed” message can have several causes, so test one thing at a time before reinstalling the app or changing your account.

First notice what still works. Can you open your profile, view Stories, or load Instagram in a browser? That helps separate a feed problem from a broader login or connection issue. Save any post you were drafting somewhere else before you troubleshoot.

Is your internet connection working?

Open another app or website on the same phone. If those also struggle, switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or try a reliable Wi-Fi network. Then return to Instagram and pull down once to refresh. Avoid tapping refresh repeatedly while the connection is unstable.

If Instagram works on one connection but not another, restart the affected network connection or check whether a VPN or content filter is interfering. You don’t need to change your account settings just because one network is having trouble.

Does Instagram load on another device or in a browser?

Try your account on another device or at instagram.com. If the feed loads there, focus on the original phone and app. If the same problem follows your account across devices, note when it started and whether other Instagram features work.

Check that Instagram and your phone’s operating system have available updates. Then close and reopen the app and restart your phone. These steps can clear a temporary app or device problem without changing your profile.

Is the issue limited to your feed?

Check your profile, messages, and another account if you have one. If only the Home feed fails while other areas load, wait a little and try again. If you see a notice about a restriction or account review, read that notice in the app. A feed error by itself doesn’t confirm an account restriction.

When multiple Instagram features stop working across devices and connections, the issue may be temporary on Instagram’s side. Ask someone else to check the same feature, then pause and retry later. Don’t share your password with a service that claims it can repair your account.

Should you clear app data or reinstall Instagram?

Try the simpler checks first: switch connections, restart the app, update it, and restart the phone. On Android, clearing the app cache may help with a local loading issue. Menu names vary by device. On iPhone, reinstalling is a later step, after you’ve saved any drafts you still need.

If you reinstall, sign in only through Instagram’s official app or website. If the feed still fails, report the problem from Instagram’s in-app help option. Include your device, app version, what you tried, and whether another device could load the feed.

What should a business do while the feed is down?

If you’re preparing a business post, keep the approved caption and image in your content folder. Check your business profile directly before assuming a scheduled or published item is missing. Avoid posting duplicates until you know whether the original appeared.

Common questions

Why is my Instagram feed not refreshing?
The app may be having trouble loading new posts because of a connection, app, device, or temporary service issue. Test another connection and reopen the app first.

What does “Couldn’t refresh feed” mean?
Instagram could not load fresh feed content at that moment. The message does not identify one cause by itself, so test the connection, app, and another device before drawing a conclusion.

Will reinstalling Instagram delete my posts?
Your published posts are tied to your account, but save any unsent drafts before reinstalling. Drafts stored on a device may not be available after the app is removed.

How can I tell if Instagram is down?
Ask whether Instagram loads for someone else and try another device or connection. If the same feature fails across devices, wait and try again later.

Should I keep refreshing my Instagram feed?
Repeated refreshes are unlikely to help if the app or service is having trouble. Check the basics once, then pause before trying again.
