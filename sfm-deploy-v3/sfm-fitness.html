Common Questions

FAQs for Gym & Fitness Owners

What kind of content converts best for fitness?

Member results and testimonials are #1. Behind-the-scenes, trainer spotlights, challenge results, and class previews all perform well. Generic motivational quotes do not.

How do free trial ads work?

We create Facebook/Instagram lead ads offering a free class pass or trial week. People opt in with their name and email. Your team follows up to book them in. Simple, effective, scalable.

Do you work with personal trainers who work solo?

Absolutely. Solo trainers are some of our best clients — your personal brand IS your business, and content marketing is the perfect vehicle to build it.

What about January — can you help us capitalize on New Year's?

Yes — and we start planning for it in September. Having your Q1 campaigns planned and ready to launch on Jan 1 is how you out-market every competitor in your area.
