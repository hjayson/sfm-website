Why Are My Instagram Notifications Not Working?

J
Jayson Hines October 7, 2026 · 6 min read

If Instagram notifications stopped arriving, check two places first: Instagram’s own notification settings and your phone’s app permissions. Then look at Focus or Do Not Disturb, battery restrictions, and whether only one kind of alert is missing.

Are notifications enabled inside Instagram?

Open Instagram, go to your profile, tap the menu, and choose Notifications. Check that Pause All is off, then open the activity category you care about, such as messages, comments, or posts. Instagram lets you control these types separately, so turning on one category will not necessarily turn on every alert.

If you use more than one account, make sure you are checking the account that is missing alerts. App menus can move or change names after an update, so use the Notifications item in the profile menu if the steps look a little different on your phone.

Does your phone allow Instagram to send alerts?

Your phone has its own permission for notifications. On iPhone, open Settings, tap Notifications, choose Instagram, and check that Allow Notifications is on. You can also choose whether alerts appear on the Lock Screen, in Notification Center, or as banners. Apple explains the controls in its iPhone notification settings guide.

On Android, open Settings, tap Notifications, then App notifications and Instagram. Make sure the app and the categories you want are allowed. Android settings vary by device, and some phones label the menu differently. Google’s Android notification guide shows the general route.

Could Focus or Do Not Disturb be delaying the alerts?

Focus on iPhone and Do Not Disturb or other Modes on Android can silence or filter app notifications. Check whether one is on now or set to turn on automatically at certain hours. On iPhone, open Settings, tap Focus, and review which apps are allowed. On Android, check Do Not Disturb under Modes or Quick Settings.

The alert may still appear later in Notification Center even if it did not pop up when you expected. If you use an Apple Watch, alerts can also appear on the watch instead of the iPhone while the phone is locked.

Are some Instagram notifications missing while others work?

That usually points to a category setting or a phone alert style. Check the specific Instagram activity first. Then check whether that notification type is set to silent, has banners turned off, or is hidden from the Lock Screen. On Android, you can also look at notification history on phones that support it.

For a business account, test a direct message and a comment separately. If one arrives and the other does not, you have narrowed the problem to a notification category. That is more useful than switching everything off and on without knowing which setting changed.

What should you try when the settings look right?

Turn off battery saver or low power mode briefly and send yourself a test alert. Android’s Battery Saver can limit background activity, and device makers sometimes add their own battery controls. If the test arrives after changing that setting, adjust the app’s battery access rather than keeping every power feature disabled.

Next, update Instagram from your app store and restart your phone. Avoid deleting the app until you know your password and can sign back in. If notifications still fail after those checks, report the problem through Instagram’s Help menu. It may be a temporary app or service issue, and repeatedly changing unrelated settings will not fix that.

How can a local business keep up with customer messages?

Notifications help, but they are not a customer service system by themselves. Make a habit of checking your inbox at set times, decide who handles replies, and keep a simple record of questions that need a follow-up. Common questions

Why are Instagram notifications not coming through?
Usually, an app setting or your phone is silencing them. Check Instagram’s notification controls first, then make sure your phone allows Instagram alerts.

How do I turn Instagram notifications back on?
In Instagram, open your profile menu and choose Notifications. Turn off Pause All and check the categories you want. The exact menu wording can vary by app version.

Why do I get Instagram notifications only when I open the app?
Your phone may be limiting background activity, or a Focus, Do Not Disturb, or battery setting may delay alerts. Check those controls and test again.

Why do I get some Instagram alerts but not others?
Instagram lets you manage notification types separately. Check the setting for the specific activity, such as messages, comments, or posts, and confirm your phone has not silenced that category.

Should I reinstall Instagram if notifications still do not work?
Try updating the app and restarting your phone first. If the problem continues, sign out only if you know your login details, then use Instagram’s in-app Help option to report the issue.
