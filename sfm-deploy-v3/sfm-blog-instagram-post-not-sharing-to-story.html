Why Can't I Share an Instagram Post to My Story?

J
Jayson HinesOctober 28, 2026 · 6 min read

If you can't share an Instagram post to your Story, first check whether the original account is public and allows resharing. Then test another post. If only one post is affected, the setting may belong to its owner. If every post is affected, check your Instagram app, connection, and account status.

Are you sharing a feed post to your Story?

This guide is about taking a photo or Reel from someone's feed and adding it to your own Story. Open the post, tap the share icon below it, and look for “Add to story.” If that option is missing, don't assume your Story feature is broken. Instagram only offers the share action when the post and account are eligible.

Is the original account public?

Posts from private accounts generally can't be reshared to other people's Stories. You may be able to see the post because you follow that account, while people who don't follow it can't open the shared version. The share option can be missing for that reason.

Try a post from a public account as a quick test. If the public post can be added to your Story and the first one can't, your app is probably working. The original account's privacy or sharing controls are the better place to look.

Has the account owner allowed people to reshare posts?

A public account can still limit resharing. The owner may have turned off the option that lets other people share feed posts to Stories. Instagram changes menu labels from time to time, so the control may appear under settings related to sharing, reuse, or Stories.

If you're checking your own business account, open Instagram settings and look for the sharing controls. Make sure the audience and the post itself are appropriate for public sharing. Then ask someone who doesn't manage the account to test a post. That gives you a useful check from the viewer's side.

Does the problem affect one post or every post?

Test a few different public posts, ideally from accounts that allow resharing. One missing option usually points to the source post or its owner's settings. A missing option on every eligible post may point to your app version, account feature availability, or a temporary issue.

Also check what happens after you tap the share icon. If “Add to story” appears but the editor fails to load, that's a different problem from the option being absent. If the editor opens but publishing stalls, review your connection and app health. Our guide to fixing an Instagram Story that will not upload covers that separate upload problem.

Could your app or account be getting in the way?

Update Instagram, close and reopen it, and try once on Wi-Fi and once on mobile data. If you use more than one Instagram account, switch profiles and test again. Avoid deleting the app until you've saved any drafts you need.

If the feature used to work and now fails everywhere, look at Account Status and recent in-app notices. Follow the steps shown there if Instagram has limited a feature. If nothing explains the issue, use Instagram's in-app Help option to report it and include what you tested.

What can a local business do when resharing is unavailable?

Don't use a screenshot to work around someone's privacy choice. Ask the original creator for permission, or share the public post link in your own Story with Instagram's link sticker if that option is available. Give the creator clear credit and make it easy for people to visit the original post.

For your own announcements, publish the original content on your account and check the sharing control before you need it. A simple, repeatable social media plan for your business can help your team decide what to post, who can share it, and where customers should go next.

Common questions

Why can't I share someone else's Instagram post to my Story?
The account may be private, or its owner may have turned off resharing. Test another public post to see whether the issue is limited to one source.

How do I share an Instagram post to my Story?
Open the feed post, tap the share icon below it, choose Add to story, edit if needed, and share. The option only appears when the post and account allow it.

Why is Add to story missing on only one post?
The post may be from a private account or the owner may not allow resharing. Compare it with a different public post before changing your settings.

How can I let people share my own Instagram posts to Stories?
Review Instagram's sharing controls and look for settings related to sharing and reuse or Story resharing. Menu names can change. Test the result from another account.

What can I do if Instagram still will not share the post?
Update and restart the app, try a different connection, and test another eligible post. If the Story editor opens but will not publish, save your media and check Account Status.
