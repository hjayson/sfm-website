Why Is YouTube Not Working? What to Check First

J
Jayson Hines November 11, 2026 · 6 min read

If YouTube isn't working, first figure out whether the problem affects one video, the app, or everything on your device. Try another video, switch networks, then restart and update the app. That quick check helps narrow down what to try next.

YouTube playback trouble can show up as a blank player, endless loading, an error message, or an app that won't open. YouTube's own video troubleshooting guide recommends a few practical checks. Work through them one at a time.

How can you tell what is causing the problem?

Play a different video. Then open YouTube in a browser or on another device if one is handy. If only one video fails, the issue may be specific to that video. If the app and browser both fail on the same network, check the connection next.

Also notice the exact error message. “Something went wrong” and a video that keeps buffering may need different checks. A note or screenshot can help if you need to send feedback later.

What should you check first when YouTube won't load?

Open another site or app that uses the internet. If those also struggle, restart your Wi-Fi connection or try mobile data. For a video that starts but keeps buffering, lower the playback quality and see whether it becomes stable.

If other sites work, close YouTube and open it again. Restarting your phone, tablet, TV, or computer can clear a temporary problem. YouTube recommends restarting the app or device for some playback errors.

How do you fix a YouTube app that won't work?

Check the app store for an update, install it if one is available, then try again. On a computer, reload the page and make sure your browser is current. You can also compare the app with youtube.com in a browser. If one works and the other does not, that points to where to focus.

On a phone, use the device's normal settings to close the app or restart the device. Menu names differ between Android and iPhone, so follow the steps for your model. Avoid deleting app data unless you understand what may be removed from that device.

What if YouTube still won't play videos?

Check whether the problem follows one account, one device, or one network. If possible, try a different supported device. Keep the error text and the steps you already tried. You can use YouTube Help to look up the error or send feedback from the app.

Common questions

Why is YouTube not working on my phone?
The cause may be the connection, the app, the phone, or a single video. Try another video, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, then restart the app and phone.

Why does YouTube work in a browser but not in the app?
That points toward an app-specific issue. Update the app, close and reopen it, restart your device, and check whether the same video plays in a browser.

What should I do if only one YouTube video won't play?
Try another video and open the problem video on a different supported device or browser. If only that video fails, the issue may be limited to that video or its availability.

Can a slow internet connection stop YouTube from playing?
Yes. Try a lower video quality or a different network. YouTube recommends checking whether your connection can support the selected resolution.

What if YouTube still won't work after troubleshooting?
Write down the error message, device, and whether other videos or services work. Check YouTube Help for the matching error and send feedback from the app if the issue continues.
