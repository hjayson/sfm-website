Before we talk

Builder marketing questions, answered.

A home builder marketing agency helps people discover your company, understand your work, and make an inquiry. At SFM, the services can include photography and video, social media management, Meta ads, website improvements, and lead follow-up. We agree on the scope around your project types, service area, and capacity.

Yes. We can plan on-site photography and video in Northwest Ohio, with the schedule and locations agreed in advance. For builders and remodelers, that can include project progress, finished spaces, and interviews with your team. We confirm site access and permissions before a shoot.

Our builder marketing services are available to custom home builders, design-build remodelers, general contractors, and related trades. During the consultation, we discuss the kinds of projects you want, your service area, and whether our services fit your business.

We start with the areas you serve and the projects you want to promote, then review the audience options and advertising requirements for that campaign. The creative and inquiry questions help explain who the service is for. We do not promise that an audience filter identifies someone’s income, home ownership, or readiness to build.

The scope depends on how much content you need, shoot locations and frequency, advertising management, and website or follow-up work. We provide a written proposal that separates service fees from advertising spend and states the schedule and terms before work begins.

Timing depends on your starting point, local demand, budget, offer, and sales process. We agree on early milestones and review inquiries and booked consultations as data becomes available. A website visit or an inquiry is not a signed construction contract, and we do not guarantee a number of projects or a return on ad spend.

Bring your website and social links, the areas you serve, the types of projects you want more of, and any photos or videos you already have permission to use. If you track inquiries or advertising spend, those records help us understand your starting point.
