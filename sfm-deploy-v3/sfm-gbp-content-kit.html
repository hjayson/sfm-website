Step 5

Your Review Request Link

GBP Dashboard → Ask for reviews → Copy link → Send to every client

Go into your GBP dashboard, click "Ask for reviews", and copy the direct link Google generates for your profile. That's your review link — text it or email it to every client after you start working with them.

Hey [Name] — really glad we're working together. If you've had a good experience so far, it would mean a lot if you left us a quick Google review. Takes less than a minute: [PASTE YOUR GBP REVIEW LINK HERE] Totally no pressure — just helps other local businesses find us. Thanks!

Send this via text — text gets opened, email often doesn't. Target: 1 new review per active client per quarter minimum.
