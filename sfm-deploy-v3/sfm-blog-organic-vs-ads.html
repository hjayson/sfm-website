Organic Social Media vs. Facebook Ads: Which One Should Your Local Business Focus On First?

J
Jayson Hines Feb 21, 2025 · 4 min read

If you've ever asked "should I be posting or should I be running ads?" — you're asking the wrong question. The right question is: which one should I build first? Here's how to think about it.

What Organic Social Actually Does

Organic social media — posting on Facebook, Instagram, or Google Business without paying for reach builds trust. It's the digital equivalent of showing up, being present, and letting people get to know you. When someone looks up your business on Facebook or Instagram and your last post was from 2023, that's a red flag — even if your reviews are great.

Organic is about credibility. It's the foundation that makes everything else work better.

What Paid Ads Actually Do

Paid ads — Facebook and Instagram ads specifically — do one thing well: they get you in front of people who don't already know you. This is extremely powerful for local businesses because you can target specific zip codes, age ranges, interests, and behaviors. You're not waiting for people to find you — you're going to them.

But here's the key: ads amplify what's already there. If your profile is empty or outdated, running ads is like sending people to an empty storefront.

The Combination That Wins

The businesses we see growing fastest in the Perrysburg and Toledo area do both.🌖 They post organically consistently — not every day, but regularly — and they run targeted ads to put their best content in front of new audiences. Organic builds trust. Ads build reach. Together, they build revenue.
