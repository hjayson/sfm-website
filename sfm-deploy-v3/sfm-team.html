Founder & Owner

Jayson Hines

Founder & Lead Strategist · Sales Funnel Marketing

Jayson started Sales Funnel Marketing in 2017 with one conviction: local businesses deserve marketing that actually works. Not vanity metrics. Not recycled templates. Real content, real strategy, and results built for the communities these businesses live in.

He built SFM from the ground up — doing the shoots, running the ads, writing the copy, and showing up at every client event along the way. Today he leads the team and the strategy, while staying close enough to every account to know what's actually moving the needle.

In 2025 he published The Digital Garden — a guide for small business owners on building a real social media audience without paying to play. It's available on Amazon in paperback, hardcover, and Kindle.

Photographer

John Booth

Lead Photographer · Sales Funnel Marketing

John came up as a photographer in the print advertising world. 30 years of shooting for art directors and designers taught him to compose every frame like it had to live next to a headline.

He's SFM's photographer for the work that matters. Events where the moments don't come back twice. Real estate that has to actually sell. Food that should make you hungry just looking at it. Headshots that look like the person on a good day.

His style adapts to the room. Dramatic when the story calls for it. Clean and corporate when the brand needs that. The common thread is that every photo serves the message first, the brand second, and the photographer last.
