Why Are My Instagram Comments Not Showing?

J
Jayson Hines October 24, 2026 · 6 min read

If your Instagram comments are missing, start with the post’s comment settings and filters, then check whether the thread is loading correctly. A comment can be hidden or held back by moderation, and a display problem can look like it vanished. Work through the checks below before you repost or assume Instagram removed it.

Why are my Instagram comments not showing?

There isn’t one cause. The post owner may have limited who can comment. A filter may have caught a word or phrase. The person who wrote the comment may have deleted it, or Instagram may have removed it. Sometimes the comments simply fail to load in the app.

First, refresh the post and open the comments again. If you manage the account, look at the post’s comment controls and the account’s filtering settings. If you’re a viewer, check whether the comment appears from another device or account. These checks can narrow down the problem, though they won’t always tell you exactly what happened.

How can I check comment controls and filters?

If you own the account, review Instagram’s comment controls and moderation filters. Check whether comments are limited for that post, whether a hidden-word filter is on, and whether the commenter may be restricted. App menus change, so look for the current comment and privacy controls in Instagram’s settings rather than following an old menu path.

For a business account, check more than one post. If a customer says they commented but you can’t see it, ask which post they used and roughly when. Then inspect that thread and your filtering settings. Don’t ask them to post the same thing repeatedly. That can make a moderation problem harder to sort out.

Could an account restriction or removed comment explain it?

Yes, but don’t jump to that conclusion from one missing comment. The commenter may have deleted it, the post owner may have removed it, or a moderation setting may be affecting what appears. Instagram may also remove content that violates its rules. If you received an in-app notice, read it before taking another action.

If you’re the person who wrote the comment, check whether it appears on the post from a different view. If you manage the post, check the filters and ask the person for the exact wording. A specific example is more useful than a guess about why it disappeared.

What if Instagram comments will not load?

Try closing and reopening Instagram, switching between Wi-Fi and cellular data, and checking the same post again. See whether other comment threads load. If only one post is affected, the issue may be with that thread or its settings. If comments fail across the app, update Instagram and try again later.

If the problem continues, report it through Instagram’s in-app help flow and include what you already checked. Don’t share account passwords or verification codes with anyone offering to fix it. A social media support provider should never need those credentials to troubleshoot a public comment issue.

When should a business investigate a wider engagement problem?

Look for a repeatable pattern. Are comments missing on one post or many? Is it happening to one person or to different customers? Are likes and replies still visible? Keep notes and screenshots, with customer details covered when you don’t need them. That gives you something concrete to review with your team.

Common questions

Why are my Instagram comments not showing?
The post’s comment controls, moderation filters, account restrictions, or a loading issue may be involved. Check the settings and try the thread from another view.

Can Instagram hide comments automatically?
Instagram has comment controls and filters. Review the account’s current settings, including hidden-word filters and moderation choices.

Why can I see my comment but nobody else can?
A filter or post setting may affect whether others see it. Ask the post owner to check the thread and its comment controls.

Did Instagram delete my comment?
It may have been removed, deleted by the author, or hidden by a filter. Check for an in-app notice and review the thread before reposting.

What should a business do when customer comments disappear?
Check post controls and filters, look for a pattern across posts, and report a continuing issue in Instagram.
