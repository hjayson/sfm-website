Why Won't My Facebook Video Upload?

J
Jayson Hines October 20, 2026 · 6 min read

A Facebook video upload usually fails because of a weak connection, an app or browser problem, the video file, or a limit on the Page or account. Try a short, known-good clip on another connection or device, then check any error message before uploading the original again.

That quick test tells you a lot. If the test clip works, focus on the original file. If it fails too, look at the app, browser, network, or Page access. Change one thing at a time so you know what fixed it.

Why won't my Facebook video upload?

Start with the exact point where it stops. Does the upload never begin, stall at a percentage, finish but fail to publish, or show an error? Those are different problems. Take note of the wording and whether you are posting from a personal profile, a Page, or a scheduling tool.

Then check your connection. Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or try a reliable Wi-Fi network if the signal is weak. Avoid starting the upload while a device is changing networks. If other apps are also slow, fix the connection before changing the video.

Is the video still uploading or processing?

A video can finish transferring and still need time to process. Keep the app or browser open long enough to see whether the status changes. If the screen appears frozen, give it a little time, refresh only after you have checked whether the post appeared, and avoid starting several copies of the same upload.

Search the Page or profile for the post before trying again. A duplicate can be confusing, especially when one copy is still processing. If you see a clear failure message, save its wording because it can point to the next step.

Could the video file or export be the problem?

Play the whole file on your phone or computer. Confirm the export finished, the sound works, and the file is not damaged. If the video came from an editing app, export it again and test a shorter copy. A smaller file is a useful diagnostic because it helps separate a file problem from a connection or account issue.

Check Facebook's current upload guidance if the composer names a file or duration limit. Those requirements can change, and the message shown in your account is more useful than an old tutorial. If the original is very large, try a standard video export at a smaller size while keeping the picture and sound clear.

Is your app, browser, or connection interrupting the upload?

Update the Facebook app, close it, and reopen it. If you are using a browser, try a current version of another browser or a private window. That can help identify an extension, cached page, or browser session that is getting in the way. Keep the original video saved before clearing anything or reinstalling an app.

Also check whether the problem happens on only one device. If the same clip uploads from a computer but not a phone, focus on the phone app and its connection. If it fails everywhere, look at the file or account next.

Can Page access or account limits block a video?

When posting as a business, confirm you are using the right Page and that your account has permission to publish there. A Page role may have changed, or you may be composing from your personal profile by mistake. Check Page notifications and account status for a restriction or review notice.

Review the audience and publishing options too. Facebook's interface changes, so use the current video or Reels composer shown to your account. A post that uploaded successfully can still have limited visibility if its audience or publishing settings are wrong.

What should you do if Facebook keeps rejecting the video?

Use a short checklist: record the error, test another connection, upload a short known-good clip, then retry the original only after you know which part is failing. If the test clip works, export the original again. If it does not, try another app or browser and check Page access and account status.

Still stuck? Use Facebook's in-product help or support route and include the error wording, device, app or browser, and whether a test clip worked. That gives support something concrete to investigate.

Common questions

Why is my Facebook video stuck at 0%?
A stalled upload can point to a weak connection, an app or browser issue, or a file that Facebook cannot process. Try another network and a short test clip before uploading the original again.

How long does a Facebook video take to upload?
There is no single time that applies to every video. File size, connection speed, and processing can all affect it. If the progress has not changed for a while, check the connection and try a short test file.

What video file should I use for Facebook?
Start with a standard video export that plays correctly on your device. If Facebook rejects it, export a smaller copy in a common format and check the current upload guidance for any limits shown in the composer.

Can I upload a video to a Facebook Page from a computer?
Use the current Page posting or video composer available to your account. Facebook changes its publishing screens, so follow the options shown in your Page rather than an older walkthrough.

Why did my Facebook video upload but nobody can see it?
Check the post's audience or visibility setting, confirm you published from the intended Page, and look for a processing notice or account warning. A successful upload does not always mean the post is public.
